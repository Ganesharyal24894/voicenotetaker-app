Record — press
A press scales to 0.93 and back. Cheap, but it is the difference between a button that feels connected to your finger and one that does not. 120 ms down, 180 ms release
Recording — live level
Driven by real amplitude, not a canned loop — the whole point is that it proves the mic is hearing you. Falls back to a flat line if frames stop arriving. follows the level meter, ~20 ms per frame
Two of these loop forever in the app — scanning and the connected dot — because they report a live condition. The rest fire once on a state change. All of them should respect the platform’s reduce-motion setting, which Flutter exposes as MediaQuery.disableAnimations; when it is on, states change instantly and the two loops become static.
